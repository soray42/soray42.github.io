## How posts are written

Each post is one `.mdx` file in `content/blog/`. The front matter sets the
title, date, and summary, and the body is Markdown.

Inline math works, like $p(y \mid x) = \prod_t p(y_t \mid y_{<t}, x)$, and so
do display equations:

$$

$$
